Serena Williams MBTI: ESTJ (Executive)

Celebrity MBTI · By Brad Kam

Best fit: ESTJ

Serena Williams (athlete) is most often typed as ESTJ (The Executive) - commanding competitor who imposes order and standards.

What this typing is based on

Serena Williams consistently shows the ESTJ pattern across years of public behavior, interviews, and decisions. Type attribution is inferential - few public figures disclose formal results - so this reflects behavioral evidence, not a diagnosis.

Could Serena Williams be a different type?

Communities debate a few alternatives, usually one letter off. But the weight of the cognitive-function evidence lands on ESTJ, which is why it remains the dominant typing.

What Serena Williams reveals about ESTJ

Serena Williams is one of the more recognizable ESTJ examples. The way they make decisions and approach problems shows the ESTJ cognitive style clearly, which is why people learning about ESTJ often find this example clarifying.
