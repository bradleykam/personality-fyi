Dwayne Johnson MBTI: ESTP (Entrepreneur)

Celebrity MBTI · By Brad Kam

Best fit: ESTP

Dwayne Johnson (actor) is most often typed as ESTP (The Entrepreneur) - high-energy, action-first, reads and works any room.

What this typing is based on

Dwayne Johnson consistently shows the ESTP pattern across years of public behavior, interviews, and decisions. Type attribution is inferential - few public figures disclose formal results - so this reflects behavioral evidence, not a diagnosis.

Could Dwayne Johnson be a different type?

Communities debate a few alternatives, usually one letter off. But the weight of the cognitive-function evidence lands on ESTP, which is why it remains the dominant typing.

What Dwayne Johnson reveals about ESTP

Dwayne Johnson is one of the more recognizable ESTP examples. The way they make decisions and approach problems shows the ESTP cognitive style clearly, which is why people learning about ESTP often find this example clarifying.
