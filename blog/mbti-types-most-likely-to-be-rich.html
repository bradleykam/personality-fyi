MBTI Types Most Likely to Be Rich

Data study · 2026-06-17 · By Brad Kam

ENTJ tops the wealth ranking, with the Thinking-Judging types dominating the top. The values-first idealists (INFP, ISFP) sit at the bottom, not because they can't, but because they optimize for meaning over money.

Wealth-building tendency by type, ranked

The top of the list

The top is all drive and strategy. ENTJ (Commander) treats wealth as a campaign to win. ESTJ (Executive) builds it through relentless execution and climbing structures. ENTP (Debater) gets there through ventures and opportunism. INTJ rounds out the top with long-range, contrarian bets.

The bottom of the list

At the bottom, INFP and ISFP aren't incapable, they're uninterested in the trade. Both optimize for meaning, authenticity, and freedom over accumulation, and will walk away from money that costs them their values. INFJ sits just above them for the same reason.

How this was scored

Scored on the traits that predict wealth accumulation: drive, comfort with risk, strategic long-range thinking, and willingness to prioritize money as a goal. Directionally consistent with published income-by-type studies (TJ types report the highest incomes); this is a trait-based analysis, not a survey of bank accounts.
