LeBron James MBTI: ENTJ (Commander)

Celebrity MBTI · By Brad Kam

Best fit: ENTJ

LeBron James (NBA player) is most often typed as ENTJ (The Commander). Decisive leader on and off court with a strategic empire. ENTJ markers: vocal leadership, business expansion, willingness to make uncomfortable moves (joining Heat, owning teams).

What this typing is based on

LeBron James's public behavior across years of interviews, decisions, and writing aligns most consistently with the ENTJ cognitive profile. Type attribution for public figures is always inferential — only formal MBTI assessment is definitive, and most celebrities have never publicly disclosed their results.

The dominant typing remains ENTJ based on the weight of behavioral evidence.
