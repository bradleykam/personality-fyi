Kylie Jenner MBTI: ESFP (Entertainer)

Celebrity MBTI · By Brad Kam

Best fit: ESFP

Kylie Jenner (beauty mogul, media personality) is most often typed as ESFP (The Entertainer). Spontaneous, warm, people-energized. ESFP markers: real-time brand pivots, sensory-aesthetic excellence, comfortable improvising in front of cameras for years on end.

What this typing is based on

Kylie Jenner's public behavior across years of interviews, decisions, and writing aligns most consistently with the ESFP cognitive profile.
