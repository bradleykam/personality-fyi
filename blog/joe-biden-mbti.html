Joe Biden MBTI: ENFJ (Protagonist)

Celebrity MBTI · By Brad Kam

Best fit: ENFJ

Joe Biden (46th US President) is most often typed as ENFJ (The Protagonist). Warm, people-oriented, mission-aligned. Long career built on personal relationships and emotional steadiness. ENFJ typings dominate; some argue ESFJ given his more traditional approach to politics.

What this typing is based on

Joe Biden's public behavior across years of interviews, decisions, and writing aligns most consistently with the ENFJ cognitive profile. Type attribution for public figures is always inferential — only formal MBTI assessment is definitive, and most celebrities have never publicly disclosed their results.

Could Joe Biden be a different type?

The most commonly debated alternative typings for Joe Biden are documented in typology communities. Some typings argue for adjacent types (e.g., switching one letter), but the cognitive function evidence holds strongest for ENFJ. The dominant typing remains ENFJ based on the weight of behavioral evidence.

What Joe Biden reveals about ENFJ

Joe Biden's public profile is one of the more recognizable ENFJ examples. The dominant cognitive function (the one ENFJs default to under low load) shows up across Joe Biden's decision-making patterns, public communication style, and the kinds of problems they choose to take on. People learning about ENFJ for the first time often find Joe Biden's example clarifying.
