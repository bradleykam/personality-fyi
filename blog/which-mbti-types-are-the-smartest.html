Smartest MBTI Types, Ranked

Data study · 2026-06-17 · By Brad Kam

INTP and INTJ top the abstract-reasoning ranking, followed by the other intuitive thinkers. But 'smart' is many things, and this measures one slice: analytical, abstract problem-solving, not emotional, practical, or creative intelligence.

Abstract reasoning by type, ranked

The Smartest MBTI Ranking: How Types Score on Different Intellects

Ranked on the abstract and analytical reasoning measured here, the same four Intuitive-Thinking types lead the spectrum — but each is “smart” in a structurally different way.

  1. 1. INTP — Fluid analytical reasoning (95). Leading with Introverted Thinking (Ti), the INTP dismantles any system down to first principles and rebuilds it. Their edge is novel, precedent-free problem solving — the purest match for the kind of puzzle IQ tests reward.
  2. 2. INTJ — Systemic strategic architecture (93). The INTJ pairs Introverted Intuition (Ni) with Extraverted Thinking (Te) to build long-range models and execute against them. Their intelligence shows up as foresight and structural planning rather than raw puzzle speed.
  3. 3. ENTP — Divergent, associative intelligence (88). With Extraverted Intuition (Ne) over Ti, the ENTP generates and connects ideas faster than almost any type. They are quickest at reframing a problem and arguing it from every angle.
  4. 4. ENTJ — Applied executive intelligence (84). Te-dominant, the ENTJ organizes knowledge directly into action and outcomes. Their intelligence is expressed through decisive, efficient strategy deployed at scale.

A note on the INFJ (78): it is the highest-scoring Feeling type, but its real intelligence sits on a different axis this abstract-reasoning ranking under-weights — Ni turned on people, producing interpersonal insight and pattern-reading rather than systems logic. As the data below makes clear, “smartest” depends entirely on which intelligence you measure.

The top of the list

INTP and INTJ are the archetypal abstract reasoners: both live in systems, models, and 'why does this work' questions. ENTP and ENTJ follow, applying the same horsepower outward to arguments and strategy. These four dominate puzzle-style, theoretical intelligence.

The bottom of the list

ESFP, ESFJ, and ISFP score lower on abstract puzzles but higher on the intelligences this test ignores: reading people, acting under real-world pressure, and practical know-how.
