The ISTJ Record: What ISTJs Have Actually Done

Type record · Updated 2026-07-01 · By Brad Kam

ISTJ (The Logistician): dutiful, methodical, and suspicious of anything that hasn’t been proven. Here is where ISTJs top the charts and where they never go, from personality.fyi’s Never Have I Ever polls across all 16 types.

Least likely of all 16 types to have…

  • Traveled to another country alone — 8% of ISTJs have
  • Eaten something I couldn't identify — 33% of ISTJs have
  • Been ghosted — 33% of ISTJs have
  • Stayed friends with an ex — 17% of ISTJs have
  • Had a friend breakup that hurt more than a romantic one — 17% of ISTJs have
  • Gone to therapy — 17% of ISTJs have
  • Waved back at someone who wasn't waving at me — 67% of ISTJs have
  • Replied all to an email by accident — 25% of ISTJs have
  • Seriously considered moving to another country — 25% of ISTJs have
  • Forgiven someone who never apologized — 33% of ISTJs have
  • Broken a rule because I thought it was unjust — 25% of ISTJs have
  • Made a major purchase on impulse — 25% of ISTJs have
  • Seen a ghost (or thought I did) — 8% of ISTJs have
  • Completely reinvented myself — 17% of ISTJs have
