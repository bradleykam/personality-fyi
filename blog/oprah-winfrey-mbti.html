Oprah Winfrey MBTI: ENFJ (Protagonist)

Celebrity MBTI · By Brad Kam

Best fit: ENFJ

Oprah Winfrey (media personality, philanthropist) is most often typed as ENFJ (The Protagonist). People-focused mission-builder who orchestrates emotional resonance at scale. ENFJ markers: warm interviews, mission-driven philanthropy, ability to unify diverse audiences.

What this typing is based on

Oprah Winfrey's public behavior across years of interviews, decisions, and writing aligns most consistently with the ENFJ cognitive profile.
