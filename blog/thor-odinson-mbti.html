Thor MBTI: ESFP (Entertainer)

Fictional Character MBTI · By Brad Kam

Best fit: ESFP

Thor (from Marvel) is most often typed as ESFP (The Entertainer) - bold, spontaneous, leads from the front with raw presence.

What this typing is based on

Thor consistently shows the ESFP pattern across appearances throughout the story. Type attribution is inferential - writers rarely assign an official type - so this reflects behavioral evidence, not a diagnosis.

Could Thor be a different type?

Communities debate a few alternatives, usually one letter off. But the weight of the cognitive-function evidence lands on ESFP, which is why it remains the dominant typing.

What Thor reveals about ESFP

Thor is one of the more recognizable ESFP examples. The way they make decisions and approach problems shows the ESFP cognitive style clearly, which is why people learning about ESFP often find this example clarifying.

Frequently asked questions

What MBTI type is Thor?

Has Thor confirmed their MBTI type?

What other types could Thor be?

Some typings argue for an adjacent type (switching one letter), but the cognitive-function evidence holds strongest for the consensus type.
