Which Personality Type Is Most Likely to Have Overthought a text for more than 10 minutes?

Data ranking · Updated 2026-07-01 · By Brad Kam

Quick answer

INFJ (The Advocate) is the personality type most likely to have overthought a text for more than 10 minutes, at 100%, followed by INFP (100%) and ISFJ (79%). ESTJ is the least likely at 44%. Across all types, 66% have.

Why INFJ leads

INFJs are reserved, principled, and quietly intense about the things that matter. INFPs (idealistic, inward, and guided by personal values over convention) and ISFJs (careful, loyal, and the last to take an unnecessary risk) round out the top three. At the other end, ESTJs are organized, assertive, and comfortable taking charge of outcomes.
