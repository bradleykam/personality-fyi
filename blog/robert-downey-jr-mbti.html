Robert Downey Jr. MBTI: ENTP (Debater)

Celebrity MBTI · By Brad Kam

Best fit: ENTP

Robert Downey Jr. (actor) is most often typed as ENTP (The Debater) - quick, irreverent, improvises his way through everything.

What this typing is based on

Robert Downey Jr. consistently shows the ENTP pattern across years of public behavior, interviews, and decisions. Type attribution is inferential - few public figures disclose formal results - so this reflects behavioral evidence, not a diagnosis.

Could Robert Downey Jr. be a different type?

Communities debate a few alternatives, usually one letter off. But the weight of the cognitive-function evidence lands on ENTP, which is why it remains the dominant typing.

What Robert Downey Jr. reveals about ENTP

Robert Downey Jr. is one of the more recognizable ENTP examples. The way they make decisions and approach problems shows the ENTP cognitive style clearly, which is why people learning about ENTP often find this example clarifying.
