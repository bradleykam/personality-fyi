Kanye West MBTI: ENFP (Campaigner)

Celebrity MBTI · By Brad Kam

Best fit: ENFP

Kanye West (rapper, designer) is most often typed as ENFP (The Campaigner). Possibility-seeking creator who connects ideas across genres impulsively. ENFP markers: high-energy ideation, dramatic public swings, scattered focus. Some typings argue ESFP for the performer side.

What this typing is based on

Kanye West's public behavior across years of interviews, decisions, and writing aligns most consistently with the ENFP cognitive profile. Type attribution for public figures is always inferential — only formal MBTI assessment is definitive, and most celebrities have never publicly disclosed their results.
