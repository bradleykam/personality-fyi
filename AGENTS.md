# Personality.fyi product direction

Owner guidance recorded October 3, 2026. Apply this context to strategic advice, prioritization, feature requests, UX changes, and answer quality work throughout this project.

## Vision

Personality.fyi adds a layer of personality-based understanding to someone's life: their job, personal relationships, and professional relationships. It should help people understand real situations and decide what to do next.

## Business goal and value loop

Conversion is critical. Earn daily active use and paid subscriptions by creating enough value and curiosity that people ask more questions, get valuable answers, and return. Conversion should follow demonstrated usefulness, not additional steps or pressure.

The intended loop is: relevant life context → curiosity → a useful question → a sharp, valuable answer → another worthwhile question or reason to return → paid subscription when the value justifies it.

## Decision filter

- Evaluate every feature request through this vision, including requests that seem like small UI changes.
- Prioritize improvements that help users reach useful understanding or an actionable answer quickly.
- Be sharp and clear. Respect the user's time. Remove unnecessary steps, clicks, choices, repeated questions, and competing actions.
- Use known context about the person, job, and relationship instead of asking users to repeat it.
- Make the primary next action obvious. Keep rare maintenance and editing tasks secondary.
- Answer quality is core product work: cover the most relevant broad possibilities first, connect advice to actual context, and avoid unsupported certainty or agreeable but ungrounded answers.
- Spark curiosity through relevance and insight, not friction, filler, generic personality descriptions, or engagement for its own sake.
- Keep UI conventions consistent so users can focus on their lives rather than learning the interface.
- If a request appears to add friction or distract from this loop, explain the tradeoff and recommend a simpler way to achieve its intent. Do not silently expand the request into unrelated changes.

## Strategic evaluation

For substantive recommendations, explain whose real problem is solved, what value the user receives, how quickly they receive it, and why it supports meaningful repeat use or paid conversion. Distinguish observed evidence from hypotheses. Judge results with meaningful answers/actions, return usage, and paid conversion, not merely clicks, emails opened, or added features.

## Lifecycle email priorities

Emails should lead to a useful next action. Without a type, prompt choosing a type or taking the test. With a type, prioritize relationship and career context; with context, prompt specific questions and further exploration in the area the person uses. Articles and Never Have I Ever are secondary, only after at least seven days without recorded activity. Preserve seed content in the product, but exclude seed/internal accounts from reporting and email statistics. Unknown measurement must be labeled unavailable, never converted into a reassuring zero.
